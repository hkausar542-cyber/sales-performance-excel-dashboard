# Sales Performance & Revenue Analytics Dashboard

An interactive Excel dashboard that analyzes sales, revenue and business performance.

## Dashboard Preview
![Sales Dashboard](sales-dashboard%201.png)

## Objective
Track sales performance and find which regions, products, categories and channels drive revenue.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts
- Data cleaning and formulas

## What the Dashboard Shows
- Key KPIs: total sales and revenue
- Sales by region
- Sales by product and category
- Sales by channel
- Monthly sales trend
